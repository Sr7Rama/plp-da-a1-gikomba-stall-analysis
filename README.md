## Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Nairobi's Gikomba Market. While she regularly keeps records of daily sales and inventory in handwritten notebooks, she lacks structured analytical insights into her business's trajectory. This project analyzes 14 days of daily sales performance and current stock metrics to evaluate overall revenue growth, identify high-volume sales days, measure inventory margins, and establish data-driven restocking strategies.

## Top Three Findings
1. **Week-on-Week Growth:** Total sales grew by 8.78% from Week 1 (KES 22,100) to Week 2 (KES 24,040).
2. **Weekend Sales Dominance:** Weekend daily sales average KES 4,900, which is 81.48% higher than the weekday daily sales average of KES 2,700.
3. **Inventory Alert:** Tomatoes and Sukuma are critically low on stock (at or below reorder level), despite Sukuma having a solid 40% gross margin and Tomatoes 33.33%.
